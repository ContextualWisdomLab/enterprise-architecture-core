# ADR 0001: Establish an Enterprise Architecture Decision Plane

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-16

## Decision

The repository owns business capability, application, interface, technology,
lifecycle, assessment, objective, initiative, and transformation facts. Data
catalog, physical schema, inferred lineage, identity credentials, and project
execution remain in their existing systems.

## Consequence

The product can be deployed independently and avoids becoming a universal CWL
monolith.
